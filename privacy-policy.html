Privacy & Information

How We Protect Your Information

Dallas ADU Group respects the privacy of homeowners, property owners, and visitors seeking information about ADU planning and construction services throughout Dallas and the DFW area.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through this website, how it may be used, when it may be shared, and the choices available regarding information you provide.

Last Updated: September 16, 2026
01

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide when requesting an ADU consultation, submitting a project inquiry, contacting us by email, or communicating with us by phone.

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and telephone number
  • Property ZIP code or general project location
  • ADU type, project details, and requested services
  • Information included in messages you send to us

Basic technical information may also be collected automatically, including browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, and general website usage data.

02

How We Use Information

Information collected through this website may be used to respond to inquiries, understand ADU project needs, coordinate project communications, provide requested information, and improve website functionality and visitor experience.

Information may also be used for administrative, security, recordkeeping, website-performance, and legal-compliance purposes where appropriate.

03

Contractors & Service Providers

Because we work directly with contractors and subcontractors in our established network, information relevant to an ADU inquiry may be shared with appropriate contractor partners when necessary to evaluate, coordinate, or respond to a project request.

Information may also be provided to companies supporting website hosting, communications, analytics, form processing, security, or other business operations. Information may be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and security.

04

Cookies & Website Analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to support functionality, understand visitor activity, remember preferences, and evaluate website performance.

Most browsers allow you to control or remove cookies through browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the operation of some website features.

05

Data Security & Retention

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to help protect information submitted through this website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, coordinate project communications, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations.

06

Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Visitors should review those policies before providing personal information through an external website.

07

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for individuals seeking information about residential and multifamily ADU projects and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through this website.

08

Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us with questions about personal information previously submitted through the website or to request an appropriate correction or deletion. Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal requirements.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to our website, business practices, technologies, or applicable requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10
