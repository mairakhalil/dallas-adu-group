Local ADU Expertise for Dallas Properties

Dallas ADU Group supports homeowners and property owners planning accessory dwelling units throughout Dallas and the DFW area. We work directly with experienced contractors and subcontractors in our established network to coordinate garage conversions, detached and attached ADUs, design, permitting, and multifamily ADU projects.

Every property brings different goals, space limitations, access conditions, and construction considerations. Our approach keeps those details at the center of the process while helping property owners understand practical options for planning and developing an ADU in Dallas and surrounding North Texas communities.

From early project planning through construction coordination, our contractor network provides practical experience focused on creating functional ADU solutions for residential and multifamily properties.
