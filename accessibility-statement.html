Website Accessibility

Our Commitment to an Accessible Website

Dallas ADU Group is committed to providing a website experience that is accessible and usable for people seeking information about ADU planning and construction services in Dallas and the DFW area.

We recognize that visitors use different devices, browsers, assistive technologies, and methods of navigation. We aim to improve accessibility as our website, content, and digital features continue to develop.

Last Updated: September 16, 2026
01

Our Accessibility Efforts

We work to make website content clear, understandable, and easier to navigate for a broad range of visitors. Accessibility considerations are incorporated into the presentation and maintenance of our website.

  • Clear page headings and content organization
  • Descriptive links and navigation labels
  • Alternative text for meaningful website images
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation where practical
  • Readable text, spacing, and visual contrast
  • Responsive layouts for different screen sizes
02

Accessibility Standards

Our accessibility efforts are informed by generally recognized web accessibility practices, including guidance provided through the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Website technologies, content, browsers, and assistive tools continue to evolve, so we periodically review opportunities to improve usability and accessibility.

03

Navigation & Website Structure

The website is organized to help visitors locate information about Dallas ADU services, project planning, service areas, frequently asked questions, and contact options without unnecessary navigation steps.

We aim to use consistent navigation, meaningful headings, descriptive link text, and logical content organization throughout the website.

04

Images & Alternative Text

Meaningful images are intended to include descriptive alternative text that communicates relevant visual information to visitors using screen readers or other assistive technologies.

Decorative visual elements may be hidden from assistive technologies when they do not provide information necessary to understand the page.

05

Keyboard & Assistive Technology

We aim for important website functions, navigation links, forms, and interactive elements to remain usable without requiring a mouse where reasonably supported by the website's technology.

Visitors may also use browser accessibility settings, screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom controls, voice tools, or other assistive technologies according to their individual needs.

06

Forms & Project Inquiries

Website forms are intended to provide clear fields and instructions for visitors requesting information about garage conversions, detached or attached ADUs, design, permitting, and other ADU project needs.

If you experience difficulty using a form, you may contact us directly by phone or email to discuss your Dallas-area ADU inquiry.

07

Third-Party Content & Services

Some website features, links, embedded content, or services may be provided by third parties. Accessibility of those external platforms may be controlled independently by their respective providers.

We encourage third-party providers to offer accessible experiences, but we cannot control every accessibility feature of websites or technologies operated by others.

08

Ongoing Accessibility Improvements

We view digital accessibility as an ongoing responsibility. As website pages, ADU service information, images, forms, and other features are updated, we aim to identify and address accessibility issues where reasonably possible.

Feedback from website visitors can also help identify barriers that may not otherwise be apparent during routine website reviews.

09

Alternative Ways to Get Assistance

If a website feature or piece of content is difficult to access, contact us and describe the information or service you are trying to reach. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the requested information through an appropriate alternative method.

10

Accessibility Feedback & Assistance

If you experience an accessibility barrier while using this website, please contact us. When possible, include the page or feature involved and a brief description of the difficulty you encountered.
